Strict Examination Protocol & Anti-Cheating Notice

• Automatic Tab-Switching Detection: Navigating away from the active examination tab, opening new windows, or minimizing the browser will immediately terminate your session and record a permanent Failed — Security Violation status.

• Timer Activation: The countdown timer will strictly initiate as soon as you confirm and begin answering question 1.

• Score Sheet Release: Scores are confidential until the instructor completes grading and officially publishes the final class score sheet.
